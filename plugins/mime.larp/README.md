# Larp

**Install URL:** [manifest.json](https://xmimiez.github.io/SnowPlugins/mime.larp/manifest.json)

Open **Larp** from the shortcuts on your own profile, alongside Nighty if its shortcut is on. Edit your display name, username, avatar, banner, bio, pronouns, badges and collectibles, then tap **Save**. Larp remembers your changes per account on this device. To clear an override, empty its field and save.

Use HTTPS image links for the avatar and banner. Open **Badges**, then press and hold an official badge to unhide or hide it. Every badge starts hidden. Use **Select Stage** for Nitro, Server Boost, Gifting, Account Age, Game Time, Streaming, Game Variety, and tiered badges such as The Void when available in Discord's badge directory. Use **Select House** for HypeSquad Bravery, Brilliance, or Balance. HypeSquad Events has its own tile. Drag visible badges to change their order, then close the picker and tap **Save**.

Enter numeric Discord Shop SKU IDs in the **Avatar Decoration**, **Profile Effect**, **Profile Border**, and **Nameplate** fields. Discord calls profile borders *profile frames*.

Larp changes how your own account looks in supported Discord views. The real profile and messages stay on Discord as they were. Other users see your real profile.

## Network

- The HTTPS image hosts you enter provide the avatar and banner images.
- Discord's badge directory and collectibles catalog supply artwork metadata. Larp reads them when you open the badge picker or enter a new collectible SKU.
- Discord serves badge and decoration artwork. The archived Last Meadow Online badge uses an image from the pinned `mezotv/discord-badges` archive when Discord's directory lacks it.
